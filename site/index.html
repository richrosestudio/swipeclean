    Take out the Trash

    Swipe through files one at a time and trash only what you do not need.

    Free to use

    About Take out the Trash

    How your files are handled

    Take out the Trash runs on your computer. It does not upload your files, and it does not delete anything until you say so. Even then, files go to Trash, not into the void.

    You choose the folder

    A scan starts only after you pick a folder. It will not wander the rest of your disk on its own. System locations cannot be chosen as the starting folder. If you pick Applications, you will get a warning, and files that belong to the OS or are in use are skipped.

    Nothing moves while you review

    • You see one file at a time. Swipe right to keep it, left to queue it. Keyboard arrows do the same.
    • Queueing is not deleting. Unreviewed files are left alone. You can undo a swipe and take files out of the queue.
    • When you are ready, you confirm. A second prompt asks you to move the queue to Trash, and you can cancel.

    Trash, not permanent delete

    Confirmed files go to Trash. On a Mac that is done through Finder, so Put Back is available, including from Take out the Trash, back to the original folder.

    Emptying Trash in Finder permanently deletes those files. Take out the Trash never empties Trash for you.

    Always skipped, and you cannot turn this off

    • macOS system locations, including System and Library, and core system folders.
    • Important data in your home Library: Keychains, Mail, Preferences, Safari, Application Support, Accounts, and app containers.
    • Files that are open in another app.
    • Version control folders named .git, .svn, or .hg.
    • Trash itself.

    Protected files are skipped at scan time, and they are checked again if something tries to move them to Trash. You can see a list of why files were skipped.

    Also skipped unless you change it

    Files modified in the last 24 hours are skipped by default, so recent work is left alone. You can shorten that window or turn it off. You can add your own protected folders, file types, and folder names. Those files never enter swipe or duplicate review.

    Duplicates

    For identical files you keep one copy and may queue the extras. For similar images, nothing is queued until you choose extras yourself.

    If a file cannot be moved

    Permission problems, missing files, in-use files, and protected files stay where they are. Failed items are listed and remain queued so nothing disappears quietly.

    Privacy

    There is no account. File names and contents are not sent anywhere. Scanning, previewing, and trashing all happen on your machine. This website cannot see the files on your computer.

    What still depends on you

    If you queue the wrong file and confirm, it goes to Trash. Put it back from the app or from Finder before you empty Trash. Go folder by folder if you are unsure. The app shows you each file so you can look before you queue.

    Open source

    Take out the Trash is open source under the MIT license. Anyone can read the code, check how it works, or send improvements.
